- Post from your **personal** LinkedIn (not company page) — judges want to see the individual builder.

- Tag `Google Cloud` and `Hack2skill` company pages so it shows in their feed.
- Post **early** (as soon as your Cloud Run URL is live). Do not wait until final submission.
